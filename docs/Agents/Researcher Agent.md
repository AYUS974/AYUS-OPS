> **File:** `src/agents/researcher.js` (3.4 KB)

---

## What the Researcher Does

The Researcher agent is a utility agent used for:
- Web search and information gathering
- Market research and competitor analysis
- Summarizing findings into structured reports

Unlike the department agents, the Researcher is typically invoked on-demand (by AYUS or another agent) rather than on the daily cron schedule.
